Access Control Policy

This Access Control Policy establishes requirements for managing user access to company systems, applications, networks, and sensitive information. The purpose of this policy is to reduce unauthorized access risks, protect confidential data, and support security and compliance objectives.

1. Scope
• This policy applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, interns, and third parties with access to company systems or information.
• The policy covers all systems, applications, cloud services, financial platforms, email systems, and network resources.
2. Access Control Principles
• Access to systems and data must follow the principle of least privilege.
• Users should only receive access necessary to perform their job responsibilities.
• Role-based access control (RBAC) should be used where possible.
3. User Provisioning
• User accounts must be approved by management before access is granted.
• Each user must have a unique account and login credentials.
• Shared accounts should be avoided unless specifically authorized and documented.
• Access rights must be documented and assigned according to business need.
4. User Deprovisioning
• User access must be removed promptly following termination, resignation, or role changes.
• Inactive accounts should be reviewed and disabled when no longer required.
• Periodic reviews should be conducted to identify unnecessary or excessive access.
5. Authentication Requirements
• Passwords must comply with the organization’s Password Policy.
• Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled for critical systems where available.
• Default passwords must be changed immediately upon account creation.
6. Privileged Access
• Administrative or privileged access must be restricted to authorized personnel only.
• Privileged accounts should be used only when administrative actions are necessary.
• Privileged access activities may be logged and monitored.
7. Access Reviews
• Management and system owners should perform periodic access reviews.
• Access reviews should verify that user permissions remain appropriate for job responsibilities.
• Excessive or unnecessary permissions should be removed.
8. Remote Access
• Remote access to company systems must use approved and secure methods.
• Users accessing systems remotely must follow company security requirements.
• Public or unsecured Wi-Fi networks should be avoided unless secure VPN access is used.
9. Monitoring and Logging
• Access to critical systems may be logged and monitored to detect unauthorized activity.
• Security logs should be reviewed periodically where feasible.
10. Incident Reporting
• Users must immediately report suspected unauthorized access, compromised accounts, or suspicious system activity.
11. Enforcement
• Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, suspension of access privileges, termination of employment, or legal action.
12. Policy Review
• This policy should be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to address operational, technological, or regulatory changes.

All personnel with access to company systems are responsible for complying with this Access Control Policy.
