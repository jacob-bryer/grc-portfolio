Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

This Acceptable Use Policy establishes guidelines for the proper use of company systems, devices, networks, applications, email, and internet resources. The purpose of this policy is to protect organizational data, reduce cybersecurity risks, and ensure compliance with security and operational requirements.

1. Scope
• This policy applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, interns, and temporary personnel with access to company systems or information.
• The policy covers company-owned devices, personal devices used for work purposes, cloud services, email systems, and internet access.
2. Acceptable Use
• Users may use company systems for authorized business purposes only.
• Limited personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with business operations, security, or productivity.
• Users must comply with all company security policies and procedures.
3. Prohibited Activities
• Users may not share passwords or authentication credentials.
• Users may not install unauthorized software or applications.
• Users may not disable antivirus, MFA, or other security controls.
• Users may not access inappropriate, illegal, or malicious websites using company resources.
• Users may not use company systems to harass, threaten, or discriminate against others.
• Users may not attempt unauthorized access to systems, accounts, or sensitive information.
4. Password and Access Requirements
• Passwords must meet company complexity requirements.
• Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled where available.
• Users must immediately report suspected unauthorized access or compromised credentials.
• Access rights must be based on job responsibilities and least privilege principles.
5. Email and Internet Usage
• Users must exercise caution when opening email attachments or clicking links.
• Suspicious emails, phishing attempts, or potential scams must be reported immediately.
• Company email accounts may not be used for unauthorized business or personal gain.
6. Data Protection
• Sensitive company, customer, employee, and financial information must be protected from unauthorized disclosure.
• Confidential data may only be stored on approved company systems or platforms.
• Users may not transfer sensitive information to personal storage devices or personal email accounts without authorization.
7. Monitoring and Privacy
• The company reserves the right to monitor the use of company systems, devices, networks, and communications.
• Users should not expect privacy when using company-owned resources.
8. Incident Reporting
• Users must immediately report lost devices, suspected malware infections, phishing attempts, or security incidents to management or IT personnel.
9. Enforcement
• Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including suspension of access privileges, termination of employment, or legal action where applicable.
10. Policy Review
• This policy should be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to address changes in technology, business operations, or security requirements.

By using company systems and resources, users acknowledge that they understand and agree to comply with this Acceptable Use Policy.
