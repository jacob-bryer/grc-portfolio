# GRC Risk & Compliance Portfolio

This repository contains practical governance, risk, and compliance work, including risk assessments, control evaluations, policy development, framework mapping, and remediation planning.

The portfolio is organized around three fictional business scenarios across different industries. Each scenario focuses on a different area of GRC work and is designed to show how security risks can be identified, assessed, prioritized, and communicated in a business-focused way.

## What's Included

### 1. Manufacturing — NIST CSF Gap Assessment

`1-manufacturing-nist/`

* **README.md** — business scenario, scope, and assumptions
* **NIST-CSF-Gap-Analysis.xlsx** — assessment of current controls against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework
* **Remediation-Roadmap.xlsx** — prioritized action plan based on identified gaps

### 2. Medical Clinic — HIPAA Security Assessment

`2-medical-clinic-hipaa/`

* **README.md** — business scenario, scope, and assumptions
* **HIPAA-Security-Assessment.pdf** — assessment based on HIPAA Security Rule concepts
* **Quantified-Risk-Register.xlsx** — risk register with scoring, likelihood, impact, and estimated financial exposure

### 3. Retail — Cyber Insurance Readiness

`3-retail-cyber-insurance/`

* **README.md** — business scenario, scope, and assumptions
* **Insurance-Readiness-Report.pdf** — review of cyber insurance readiness and common underwriting expectations
* **Policies/**

  * **Acceptable-Use-Policy.pdf** — sample employee technology use policy
  * **Incident-Response-Plan.pdf** — sample incident response plan for a small business environment

## Why Three Business Scenarios

Each folder represents a separate fictional engagement with its own industry context. The manufacturing scenario focuses on framework-based gap analysis, the medical clinic scenario focuses on regulatory security requirements and risk quantification, and the retail scenario focuses on cyber insurance readiness, policy, and incident response planning.

This structure is meant to show range instead of repeating the same type of assessment across multiple examples.

## Why I Built This

I built this portfolio to demonstrate practical GRC skills beyond certifications. The goal is to show that I can review a business environment, identify security and compliance gaps, assess risk, recommend realistic improvements, and present findings in a format that is useful to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Disclaimer

All company names, findings, risks, and scenarios in this portfolio are fictional and created for demonstration purposes only. No real client, employer, or confidential information is included.
