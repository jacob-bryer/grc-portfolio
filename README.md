# GRC Risk & Compliance Portfolio

This repo holds the practical GRC work I've put together — risk assessments, control evaluations, policy writing, and framework mapping. Instead of one generic writeup, I built it as three separate business scenarios across different industries, each one showing a different piece of how I actually work through risk and compliance.

## What's in here

### 1. Manufacturing — NIST CSF Gap Assessment
`1-manufacturing-nist/`
- **README.md** — business scenario and scope
- **NIST-CSF-Gap-Analysis.xlsx** — shows I can assess an environment against a framework
- **Remediation-Roadmap.xlsx** — shows I can turn identified gaps into an actual action plan

### 2. Medical Clinic — HIPAA Security Assessment
`2-medical-clinic-hipaa/`
- **README.md** — business scenario and scope
- **HIPAA-Security-Assessment.pdf** — shows I understand regulatory/compliance requirements
- **Quantified-Risk-Register.xlsx** — shows I can score risk and tie it to financial impact

### 3. Retail — Cyber Insurance Readiness
`3-retail-cyber-insurance/`
- **README.md** — business scenario and scope
- **Insurance-Readiness-Report.pdf** — shows I understand cyber insurance readiness requirements
- **Policies/**
  - **Acceptable-Use-Policy.pdf** — shows I can write policy
  - **Incident-Response-Plan.pdf** — shows I can plan an actual response

## Why three verticals
Each folder is its own fictional engagement with its own industry context — manufacturing, healthcare, retail — so the portfolio shows range instead of just one type of assessment done three different ways. Manufacturing leans on framework gap analysis, healthcare leans on regulatory compliance and risk quantification, retail leans on insurance readiness and policy/incident planning.

## Why I built this
I wanted a portfolio that proves I can do the actual work — walk into an environment, figure out what's wrong, rate the risk honestly, recommend something realistic, and write it up in a way that's useful to people who aren't security specialists. Certifications show I know the material; this is meant to show I can apply it.

## Disclaimer
Every company name, finding, and scenario in this portfolio is fictional and built for demonstration purposes only. No real or confidential client information appears anywhere in this repo.
